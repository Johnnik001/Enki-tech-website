## Company post

**CRA reporting starts on 11 September. Is your technical workflow actually ready?**

Article 14 of the EU Cyber Resilience Act applies from 11 September 2026.

ENISA says the CRA Single Reporting Platform is scheduled to become operational on the same date for mandatory manufacturer notifications. Its latest guidance also confirms that Assigned Representative users authenticate through EU Login and need MFA to access the platform.

That makes the readiness question much more concrete.

If the reporting clock started during a real event, could your team answer these five questions quickly?

• What is the authoritative awareness timestamp?
• Who owns the 24-hour and 72-hour workflow — and who is the backup?
• Can the intended SRP users actually access the platform through EU Login with MFA?
• Can product, impact, mitigation and evidence data be retrieved from named source systems in time?
• Does the workflow continue into owned remediation, technical verification and closure evidence?

A policy, scanner, SBOM, ticketing tool and reporting portal do not automatically create a working operational path.
