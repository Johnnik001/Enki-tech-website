## Qualification questions
1. Where did the findings originate — audit, pentest, Microsoft recommendation, internal assessment or customer requirement?
2. Which Microsoft services are involved?
3. How many findings are considered material enough to act on now?
4. Who owns implementation?
5. Who can approve exceptions or residual risk?
6. Are acceptance criteria already defined?
7. Is there a fixed regulatory, audit, customer or project deadline?
8. What change-control constraints exist?
9. What evidence is required to demonstrate closure?
10. Is this direct delivery or partner-led delivery?
