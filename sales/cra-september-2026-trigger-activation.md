# CRA September 2026 — Sales Trigger Activation

Date: 2026-09-10

## Trigger

CRA Article 14 applies from 11 September 2026. ENISA says the Single Reporting Platform is scheduled to become operational on the same date for mandatory manufacturer notifications. ENISA FAQ updated 9 September documents personal EU Login/MFA and recommends SRP registration when a real notification is needed. Use offline templates for exercises; do not submit dummy notifications or promise an SRP API integration.

## ICP

Use only for organisations likely to be manufacturers of products with digital elements or partners serving them:
- manufacturers of software products placed on the EU market; assess SaaS and remote data-processing scope individually
- industrial technology / connected-device manufacturers
- cybersecurity product vendors
- embedded / IoT / OT product manufacturers
- product-security consultancies, MSSPs and integrators serving these buyers

Do not use this trigger for generic enterprises where CRA manufacturer obligations are not relevant.

## Buyer roles

- Head of Product Security / PSIRT
- CISO / Security Engineering lead
- CTO / VP Engineering
- Head of Product / Platform Engineering
- Compliance / regulatory owner paired with a technical stakeholder
- Cybersecurity consultancy / MSSP partner lead

## Direct-buyer opener

> CRA Article 14 starts applying on 11 September, and ENISA has now published the operational SRP access guidance. I’m not reaching out with a legal-compliance pitch. The question I’m testing with manufacturers is operational: if T0 happened now, could Product, Security and Engineering preserve the awareness time, retrieve the required evidence, access the SRP and move the issue into owned remediation inside the reporting workflow? Enki Tech runs a bounded technical dry run for exactly that path.

CTA: send the dry-run checklist first; propose a 20-minute fit check only after interest.

## Partner opener

> With CRA Article 14 becoming applicable on 11 September, some product-security clients may discover that the advisory work is clear but the technical workflow is not: ownership, evidence retrieval, SRP access and remediation handoffs. Enki Tech can sit under a partner-led engagement and run a bounded technical dry run or take resulting Microsoft Cloud / identity remediation work to verified closure.

## Commercial path

**Insight → checklist → CRA Incident Reporting Readiness Sprint → technical remediation → Continuous Security Assurance**

## Qualification questions

1. Are you acting as a manufacturer of products with digital elements, or advising one?
2. If an Article 14 event occurred now, where is the authoritative awareness time recorded?
3. Who owns the 24/72-hour workflow and backup submission path?
4. Are personal EU Login/MFA, representative roles and backup handoffs prepared, and is ENISA’s registration guidance understood?
5. What evidence or product data still requires manual correlation?
6. Who owns technical remediation after reporting?

## Success signal

Strong evidence is a real dry-run request, a confirmed operational gap, or a partner asking Enki Tech to support a manufacturer work package. Do not build additional CRA product features until these appear repeatedly.
